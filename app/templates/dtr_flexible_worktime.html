Set quincena-specific flexible schedules saved to flexible_worktime. Used during Regenerate DTR. Assign date ranges per employee — including weekends for night-shift units (e.g. MDRRMO). Split duty uses In / Break-out / Break-in / Out. Straight duty is continuous In → Out (no meal break; typically 8 hours; two DTR punches). Rest marks scheduled day(s) off (0 worktime, REST DAY — no duty times). For Rest, click each rest day on the quincena calendar (several non-consecutive dates in one add) or use Date from / Date to to mark a contiguous block. When Out is earlier than In (e.g. 4:00 pm → 12:00 am, or 10:00 pm → 6:00 am), the shift is overnight. Date range = shift-start days only (not the morning checkout date). Example night crew: Mon Rest; Tue–Thu Straight 4:00 pm → 12:00 am; Fri–Sun Straight 10:00 pm → 6:00 am (Date to = last start day — do not extend into the next morning). Different slots must not share calendar dates. Put both In and Out punches on that start-day DTR row (Out may be an early-morning clock time). Employees not on this list use 5-day / 4-day compressed rules (weekends and 4-day rest Fridays are not counted even if DTR has entries). {{ period_label }}
