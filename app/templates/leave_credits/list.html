Leave Credits

Workweek calendar

Defaults to the current calendar month. To accrue the previous calendar month instead, choose “Previous calendar month (clear year)” in the month list and clear the year field. VL/SL rows use the last day of that month. January accrual also posts Jan 1 grants of 3 SPL and 5 WL (for anyone appointed before that Jan 1; same-year Jan 1 hires get those only via the appointment ledger row). December accrual posts Dec 31 rows to zero out unused SPL/WL. New SPL/WL on appointment (3 + 5) are recorded only when an eligible employee’s appointment date is in the current calendar year — prior-year appointment grants are not posted and are removed if present. Undo accrual removes those automated rows for the chosen period for every employee (same year/month rules as Run accrual), writes deletion snapshots for audit, and recomputes balances. It does not delete manually entered ledger lines unless they exactly match the system accrual wording and dates.
