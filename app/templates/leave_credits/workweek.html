After the year is chosen, official Philippine regular and special non-working holidays that fall on Friday flag that ISO week (Monday–Sunday), including weeks that cross December/January (for example New Year’s Day Friday 1 Jan 2027 flags the week of 28 Dec 2026).

Compressed = 4-day, 10 hours, VL/SL/LWOP 1.250 per day. Regular = 5-day, 8 hours, 1.000 per day. A flagged week is always regular; leave lines on Mon–Fri of that week are recomputed and remarked 5-day workweek. Add a local Friday holiday name and check the flag when needed.
